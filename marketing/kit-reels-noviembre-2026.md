## Especificaciones de cada Reel

- 1080 x 1920 px, 30 fps, MP4 H.264, de 15 a 25 segundos. Además, una versión de 15 s para anuncios.
- Zona segura: deja libres 250 px arriba y 350 px abajo.
- Sin música ni voz: el audio en tendencia se pone al publicar. Texto en pantalla siempre, máximo 8 palabras por pantalla.
- Segundo 0 a 1: gancho grande ya visible. Nada de logo al inicio.
- Un cambio visual cada 1.5 a 2.5 segundos.
- Cierre (últimos 3 s): Coach Banana señalando + botón tinta "Empieza 7 días gratis en bananaburn.app" + logo.
- Portada aparte de 1080 x 1920 con el gancho, legible al recortarla a 1080 x 1350.

## Reglas que no se rompen

- Colores: paper #FFF8E3, banana #FFD60A, ink #1B1505 (blanco #FFFBF0 en tarjetas).
- Solo Coach Banana, Coach Platanita y El Nuevo. Ningún otro mono.
- Capturas reales de la app, sin cambiar ningún número.
- Prohibido: emojis en el arte, testimonios inventados, contadores falsos, "últimos cupos", fotos de antes/después, mencionar inteligencia artificial, números de WhatsApp.
- Se escribe "banana", nunca "banano". Números en formato 8,000 / 1.5.
- Todo lo de GLP-1 lleva abajo: "No sustituye la indicación de tu médico."

## Los 5 videos base (prompts)

Generar cada toma por separado, de 5 a 8 segundos. Sin caras reconocibles, sin texto ni logos y sin música. Gente real y normal, no fisicoculturistas.

### Video 1: Gym en acción
```
Vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 30fps, realistic handheld-stable cinematic look. Modern Latin American gym, warm natural light, clean neutral tones with subtle yellow accents. A fit-but-ordinary adult (25-40), face out of frame or turned away, performs a controlled dumbbell goblet squat with good form. Medium shot, slow push-in. No logos, no text, no brand names, no music. Realistic body, not bodybuilder. 6 seconds.
Variations: (b) close-up of hands gripping dumbbells and lifting from rack; (c) Romanian deadlift side view; (d) seated machine chest press, over-the-shoulder shot.
```

### Video 2: Gimnasio de condominio
```
Vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 30fps, realistic. Small residential condo gym in a modern apartment building in Costa Rica: a few machines, dumbbell rack, large window with tropical greenery outside, early morning soft light. Shot 1: a hand taps a key card on the gym door reader and the door opens. Shot 2: wide shot of the quiet, empty gym. Shot 3: one resident, face not visible, starts a workout on a bench. No logos, no text, no music. 6 seconds per shot.
```

### Video 3: Corriendo en exterior
```
Vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 60fps, realistic. Sunrise run in a tree-lined urban park in Latin America (San José style), golden light. Shot 1: close-up of running shoes hitting the pavement, low angle, slight slow motion. Shot 2: runner from behind, steady tracking shot. Shot 3: runner glances at a smartphone on an armband, screen not readable. No faces visible, no logos, no text, no music. 6 seconds per shot.
```

### Video 4: Comida con proteína
```
Vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 30fps, realistic food video, top-down and 45-degree angles. Bright kitchen, cream-colored countertop, natural daylight. Shot 1: hands crack two eggs into a pan. Shot 2: top-down plate being assembled with grilled chicken, black beans, rice, avocado and salad, typical Latin American plate. Shot 3: Greek yogurt bowl with banana slices. No faces, no logos, no text, no music. 6 seconds per shot.
```

### Video 5: Celular en uso (base para capturas)
```
Vertical 9:16 video, 1080x1920, 30fps, realistic. Hands holding a modern smartphone with a solid bright green screen (#00FF00, flat, no reflections) for screen replacement. Shot 1: in a gym, person sitting on a bench scrolls the phone. Shot 2: at home on a sofa, thumb taps the screen. Shot 3: phone resting on a gym floor next to a dumbbell, screen facing camera, slow push-in. Keep the screen fully visible and unobstructed. No faces, no logos, no text, no music. 6 seconds per shot.
```
